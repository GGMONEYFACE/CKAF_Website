6th Green Belt Testing Requirements

Students must know and be able to recite all 12 Bible verses at the start of belt testing. Students must also know all previous testing requirements.

  1. 1. Pyong An Yi Dan
  2. 2. Hands and feet combinations by command, as well as hands only/feet only
  3. 3. Show proper technique and power kicking targets
  4. 4. Kicks:
    • All previously learned kicks
    • Jump spinning hook
    • Jump spinning outside crescent
    • Inside crescent
    • Butterfly Kick
  5. 5. Attacking techniques with and without a partner
  6. 6. Sparring with protective gear on
  7. 7. Two knife defence techniques
  8. 8. Phase kicks one through four
  9. 9. The first nine One-Steps
